# Frequently Asked Questions

**What is PM2.5?**
PM2.5 refers to airborne particles 2.5 micrometers or smaller in diameter. They are small enough to enter the bloodstream through the lungs and are the primary pollutant tracked during Lahore's smog season.

**What is the difference between PM2.5 and AQI?**
PM2.5 is a raw concentration measurement (µg/m³). AQI (Air Quality Index) is a standardized index (0–500) calculated from PM2.5 and other pollutants using fixed breakpoints. The two are not numerically interchangeable; AQI must be derived from PM2.5 using the correct formula, not compared directly.

**Why is Lahore's smog worse in October–December?**
Crop residue burning in surrounding agricultural areas, lower wind speeds, temperature inversion that traps pollutants near the ground, and increased vehicle emissions combine during this period.

**Do air purifiers actually help?**
Yes, HEPA-filter air purifiers reduce indoor PM2.5 meaningfully in a closed room. They do not address outdoor exposure.

**Is it safe to exercise outdoors during smog?**
Not recommended when PM2.5 is in the hazardous range. See DOC-01 for detailed guidance by pollution level.

**Who issues official smog alerts in Punjab?**
The Punjab Environmental Protection & Climate Change Department, in coordination with the School Education Department for school-related decisions.
